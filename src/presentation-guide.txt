REGRETMAP — HOW TO PRESENT (2 MINUTES)
======================================
Deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/CUJURNy2G7GGhQ6xZvVDh6  (6 slides)
Speak slowly and clearly. Total: about 2 minutes.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------
- Open the deck and press Present (full screen). Use the arrow keys to move.
- Keep the live site open in another tab: https://regret-map.shamiltp.me
- Sign in to the demo account beforehand if you plan to show it.
- Practise once with a timer.


SLIDE 1 — COVER (about 15 seconds)
----------------------------------
SAY:
"Hi, this is RegretMap. Travel smarter, regret less.
It's a community map of travel mistakes, so the next traveller
doesn't repeat them."


SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM (about 20 seconds)
----------------------------------------
SAY:
"Travel sites are great at rating hotels and restaurants.
But the mistakes that actually ruin a trip are hard to find:
going on the wrong day, like the Taj Mahal on a Friday when it's closed,
the wrong season, or packing too much into one day."


SLIDE 3 — HOW IT WORKS (about 20 seconds)
-----------------------------------------
SAY:
"It's simple. Search any place.
See every story pinned on a map, with a heatmap.
Read the lessons, react and reply in threads.
And ask questions about that destination."


SLIDE 4 — AI THAT DOESN'T MAKE THINGS UP (about 25 seconds)
-----------------------------------------------------------
SAY:
"This is the part we're proudest of: the AI doesn't hallucinate.
It only uses real published stories.
Every tip links to its sources, it never invents prices or numbers,
and if no story covers your question, it honestly says so.
For a full trip plan, it points you to The Visa Guy."


SLIDE 5 — WHAT WE BUILT (about 20 seconds)
------------------------------------------
SAY:
"We added 329 travel lessons across 53 countries, including every
Indian state. It's built with Next.js, Supabase with row-level security,
Leaflet maps and Google Gemini. It installs as an app on your phone,
and it's covered by over 300 automated tests."


SLIDE 6 — CLOSE (about 10 seconds)
----------------------------------
SAY:
"Every journey leaves a lesson. RegretMap makes sure you learn it
before you go. Try it at regret-map.shamiltp.me. Thank you!"


TIPS
----
- Look at the audience, not the screen.
- One idea per slide: don't read every word on the slide.
- If you're running long, shorten slide 5 to the three big numbers.
- If time allows a quick demo: search "Kyoto", open a story,
  then click "Ask a question" on the destination page.


IF THEY ASK QUESTIONS
---------------------
Q: Are the stories real?
A: Community stories are from real users. Our team-written travel notes
   are clearly labelled "Travel note", never shown as personal experiences.

Q: How do you stop the AI from hallucinating?
A: The server checks every answer: sources must be real stories for that
   destination, numbers must appear in those stories, and answers without
   a source are replaced with an honest "not covered" message.

Q: What if the AI is down?
A: We rotate between several API keys. If all fail, the site still works
   and the chat shows the most related stories instead.

Q: Is user data safe?
A: Every database table has row-level security, secret keys stay on the
   server, and people's emails are never shown publicly.
