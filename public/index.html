RegretMap Open the app
A project by Shamil TP

Every journey leaves a lesson.

RegretMap is a community map of travel mistakes. Travellers share what went wrong and what they'd do differently, so the next person doesn't repeat it.

Search a destination Share your story

What you can do

📍

Explore the map

Every story is a pin. Filter by country, category and season, or switch on the regret heatmap.

🧳

Before you go

Each destination gets a short summary of lessons, with every tip linked to the stories behind it.

💬

Ask travellers

A chat that answers only from that destination's published stories. No made-up advice.

😬

React & discuss

Mark stories Helpful, Yikes or Been there, and reply in threaded discussions.

How it works

  1. Search any destination, or pick one on the map.
  2. Read real experiences: what happened, what went wrong, the lesson.
  3. Plan with the Before-you-go summary and ask questions.
  4. Back home? Share your own story. It goes live right away.
